What we do, in detail

Four activities: acquiring, improving, leasing and maintaining property held for the long term.

Program 01 · Direct from owners

Acquisition

Purchases are made for the company's own portfolio: no listing, no commission, no chain of other buyers. Condition is expected rather than penalised, and the offer comes in writing after the property has been seen in person.

Program 02 · Built to last

Capital works

Because the property is being kept, works are specified for durability rather than for photographs. Electrical, plumbing, roofing and structural work goes to licensed trades, permits are obtained where required, and documentation is retained for the file.

Program 03 · Written, and consistent

Leasing

Properties are leased directly, with consistent application criteria and compliance with fair housing law. The lease sets out both parties' obligations in language a tenant can actually read, and availability is listed here when there is any.
