How we work

Buy direct, build properly, lease fairly

Buying directly removes commission, the listing period and the chain of buyers who may not close. For an owner with a deadline, certainty often matters more than the headline figure.

Capital works then get specified for durability. Licensed trades and permits cost more at the time and far less over the years the property stays in the portfolio.

And leasing has to be consistent: the same criteria for everyone, a written lease in plain language, and repairs that are actually answered.
