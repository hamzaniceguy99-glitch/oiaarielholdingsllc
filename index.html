How we work

A principal, not an agency

OIA Ariel Holdings LLC is a limited liability company registered in the State of Florida, with registration details on public record with the Florida Division of Corporations. It buys and holds property for its own account.

It is not a licensed real estate brokerage: it represents no buyer, seller or tenant, charges no commission, and gives no real estate, legal or tax advice. If you want representation or advice, engage a licensed broker or an attorney and we'll deal with them directly. Nothing on this site is an offer of any security or investment.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Are you an agency?

No. We're a buyer and a landlord acting for our own account — not licensed brokers, representing nobody, charging no commission.

Will you list my property?

No, we'd be buying it. If a listing suits you better, a licensed broker is the right call and we'll say so.

Do you buy properties needing work?

Yes, routinely. You don't need to repair or clean anything before we look.

How do you handle applications?

The same criteria for every applicant, in line with fair housing law, and we'll tell you the criteria before you apply.

Can I invest alongside you?

No. The company invests its own capital and makes no offer of any investment or security.

Who does your repairs?

Licensed trades, with permits where the work requires them. We keep the records for each property.

Selling, or looking for a place?

Tell us about the property. We reply within one business day, with no obligation.
